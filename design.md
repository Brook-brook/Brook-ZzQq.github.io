# Brook’s Digital Universe

The locked direction is an open personal digital garden. Learning, projects,
creative experiments and future interests may grow without a permanent ranking.
Brook’s Atlas is the campus-map work within this wider space. Never infer
biography, achievements or progress from demo content or current interests.

Obsidian Glass is the user’s supplied brand: #080710 base, #181225 elevated,
#A78BFA violet, #D8C9FF soft lavender, #F5F1FF text. All actual colors, typography,
spacing, glass depths and motion are in assets/css/atlas-tokens.css; optional light
mode stays in the same family. No external font or UI/motion framework is needed.

Home uses an actual, operable tutorial notebook, one real campus product capture,
a reading index, runnable experiments and source-backed build records. Study and
Projects are content indexes, not marketing landing pages. Do not invent sections
or data to satisfy a generic landing-page recipe. Keep every established route.

Glass depth identifies floating controls, notebook and selected information,
never coats every content row or the full map. User-requested spring selection,
bounded magnetic action and isolated pointer light have meaningful jobs and stop
under reduced motion or unsupported pointer capabilities. Real tutorial titles
may wrap to two phone lines; primary navigation and control labels remain single-line.

See docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md for the shared system, docs/iteration-5-audit.md for scope,
and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for provenance. Every iteration updates its compliance
report. No production source changes, auto-merge or deployment without authorization.

## Lavender garden refinement (Iteration 7)

The user's latest preference refines the material to pale/deep purple, translucent
frosted glass and a soft, comforting personality. Authoritative colors are now
#171127 night canvas, #231A39 elevation, #C3A5F5 action and #E3D4FF soft accent;
light mode is #F5EFFC with #71439F actions and dark plum text. Tokens remain the
source of truth. The older hex examples above describe the previous iteration.

Keep the actual personal notebook, learning content and campus work. Round actions
into pills, retain readable fields and use existing Mote only as a tiny companion.
Glass is isolated and has opaque/reduced-transparency fallbacks. Never introduce
idle character animation, fake personal progress, brand logos or proprietary fonts.

Design specification order: atmosphere, semantic palette, type hierarchy,
component states, layout/spacing, elevation, guardrails, responsive behavior and
implementation guidance. See docs/design-research-iteration-7.md for the pinned
reference study; this is an original Brook adaptation, not a copied brand theme.
